      reasoning behind the move
    pros:
      - Answers directly at a normal pace
      - Maintains eye contact and vocal clarity
      - Treats the dismissive comment as an opening for calm clarification
      - Corrects weakening behaviors the moment they appear
    cons:
      - Requires noticing your own pressure signals in real time
      - Can feel effortful before it becomes natural
  - id: b
    content: >-
      Apologize for taking the conversation there, then wrap up the explanation
      quickly
    pros:
      - Defuses the immediate awkwardness
      - Ends the uncomfortable moment faster
    cons:
      - Apologizes for a legitimate interest
      - Speeds up the answer under pressure
      - Signals discomfort through observable behavior
      - Reinforces habits that weaken under pressure
  - id: c
    content: >-
      Respond with enthusiasm, laying out every benefit of the new field to win
      them over
    pros:
      - Communicates genuine interest in the field
      - Keeps vocal energy and engagement high
    cons:
      - Turns the answer into a sales pitch
      - Can read as overcompensating for doubt
      - Loses the calm, direct tone the framework favors
  - id: d
    content: >-
      Keep the answer brief and vague, then steer the conversation back to safer
      ground
    pros:
      - Avoids visible nervousness in the moment
      - Buys time to collect your thoughts
    cons:
      - Dodges the direct answer the framework calls for
      - Leaves the dismissive comment unaddressed
      - Lets weakening behaviors go uncorrected
